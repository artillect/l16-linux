postmarketOS v26.06 with Phosh on the Light L16, now on systemd, postmarketOS's default for Phosh. Settings' switches for SSH and file sharing work, and the shell turns with the camera while Viewfinder stays put.

> [!WARNING]
> In-development port: flashing it can leave your camera unable to boot, and it runs lightly tested drivers. [Back up your stock partitions](https://github.com/artillect/l16-linux/wiki/Unlocking-and-backups) first. No warranty.

**Install:** [Dual boot](https://github.com/artillect/l16-linux/wiki/Dual-boot) keeps Android alongside Linux (it needs a `linux` partition); [Installation](https://github.com/artillect/l16-linux/wiki/Installation) replaces Android with Linux, and Android can be put back later. Either way you need an unlocked bootloader.

**Upgrading from 0.1.x is a reinstall:** an installed system can't change its init system, so `apk upgrade` won't bring you to 0.2.0. Copy your photos off first (they're in `~/Pictures/L16`), then install 0.2.0 the same way you installed 0.1.0; it replaces the Linux side, and Android isn't touched. 0.1.x keeps working, but its package repository is frozen: it gets no more updates. As far as we know, this is the last update that needs a reinstall: from 0.2.0 on, updates come with `sudo apk upgrade`.

**Log in** as `user` with password `147147`, and change it right away. SSH is off; turn it on with the SSH switch in Settings after changing the password.

## What's new

**systemd** in place of OpenRC, as postmarketOS ships Phosh by default:
- Settings' SSH switch works, and so does File Sharing over USB (the firewall keeps it off Wi-Fi).
- Low memory is handled by systemd-oomd with zram swap, and the firewall is postmarketOS's nftables one.

**Rotation:**
- The status bar, home bar and lock screen turn with the camera, while Viewfinder stays in landscape and turns its own controls, as stock does.
- Viewfinder's dials and mode wheel swipe across the screen in portrait too.
- The lock screen fits the screen in either orientation, without the camera showing around it.

**Viewfinder and Lightbox:**
- Lightbox: swipe between photos as on a phone, and rotate photos left or right.
- Both show up in GNOME Software's search.
- Viewfinder: a half press focuses at the last focus point; no stutter with geotagging on and location services off.

**The rest of the system:**
- Display scale 200% by default.
- Smoother scrolling: the display no longer drops a frame in six.
- Sound comes up on every boot.
- The accelerometer, gyro and light sensor keep working after long sleeps.
- The camera modules power off after 10 seconds idle, and the live preview uses less power.

## Known issues

- No video recording yet.
- Rendering a photo takes about 15 to 25 seconds.
- Not working yet: the 3.5 mm microphone jack, USB OTG and DisplayPort, the proximity sensor beside the screen.
- Viewfinder is the camera app to use: PipeWire apps such as Snapshot don't see the cameras.
- Photos don't show up on a computer as a camera when plugged in yet; use File Sharing or SSH.
- Settings' Automatic Login switch doesn't work (the accounts service can't set it up for this login screen).
- The first GPS fix after installing can take about 10 minutes; later ones are much quicker.
- Lightbox's copies only last while Lightbox is open, and there's no share option yet.
