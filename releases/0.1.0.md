The first release with the cameras: postmarketOS v26.06 with Phosh on the Light L16, dual-booted with the stock LightOS, now taking photos with all 16 modules.

> [!WARNING]
> In-development port: flashing it can leave your camera unable to boot, and it runs lightly tested drivers. [Back up your stock partitions](https://github.com/artillect/l16-linux/wiki/Unlocking-and-backups) first. No warranty.

**Install:** see [Installation](https://github.com/artillect/l16-linux/wiki/Installation). You need an unlocked bootloader and a `linux` partition.

**Upgrading from 2026.09.27:** `sudo apk upgrade`, then `sudo apk add l16-camera l16-gallery`, and restart.

**Log in** as `user` with password `147147`, and change it right away. SSH is off; turn it on with `sudo rc-update add sshd default` after changing the password if you want it.

## What's new

**Viewfinder**, the camera app (laid out after OpenLight):
- Photos from every module for the zoom, saved as LRI files like stock's; a live preview from the 28 and 70 mm modules, cropped up to 150 mm.
- Auto, ISO priority, shutter priority and manual, with EV, on stock's mode wheel; tap focus and AF-D; flash; metering; timer, burst, grid, histogram; zoom on the touch strip.
- White balance presets from each camera's own factory calibration.
- Stock's assists: tripod mode and stacked low-light shots, the hand-shake warning, the lens-blocked warning, overheating, battery and storage status.
- Portrait: the controls and text turn with the camera, as stock's do, and portrait photos come out upright.
- Geotagging (a setting), with the camera's own GPS.
- In a pocket (lenses covered in the dark): a countdown, then the camera goes to sleep.

**Lightbox**, the gallery: photos by day, a quick look straight from the LRI, and the full photo rendered by Light's own renderer (from the stock partitions) when you ask. It shows where a geotagged photo was taken, can copy the LRI or the render or show either in its folder, and deletes several photos at once.

**The image's apps:** Viewfinder and Lightbox come installed and in the favourites, with postmarketOS's usual apps (Firefox, Files, Image Viewer, Maps, Software, file sharing and the rest) but without the phone ones (Calls, Chats, Contacts) or the Snapshot camera app. Anything else is an `apk add` away.

**The rest of the system:**
- GPS from the modem's receiver, with XTRA assistance, for any app through geoclue.
- Deep sleep: the camera now reaches its lowest-power state in standby.
- The five proximity sensors around the lenses; photos dated with the real time.

## Known issues

- No video recording yet.
- Rendering a photo takes about 15 to 25 seconds.
- Not working yet: the 3.5 mm microphone jack, USB OTG and DisplayPort, the proximity sensor beside the screen.
- Viewfinder is the camera app to use: PipeWire apps such as Snapshot don't see the cameras.
- After many hours, the accelerometer, gyro and light sensor can stop updating; restarting brings them back.
- Lightbox's copies only last while Lightbox is open, and there's no share option yet.
